Privacy Policy

PayNova Demo Store · Last updated: 26 October 2026

PayNova Demo Store is a demonstration payment store. This policy explains what data it handles when you use the demo checkout.

What we collect

  • Details you enter at checkout: your name, phone number and email address, and the order amount.
  • Order and payment status for that checkout session.
  • Bank payment observations only when the merchant has paired an observer: the SMS observer reads only matching bank/UPI credit notifications on the paired phone, and the Gmail observer reads only the merchant's bank-alert mailbox. Neither reads your personal inbox or full SMS history.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not store your UPI PIN, bank password or card details. UPI payments are completed in your own UPI app.

Demo data

This is a demo deployment. Its database resets on every redeploy, so demo settings and test records do not persist.
